PAYMENT & DELIVERY POLICY

At NELSMART Electronic, we are committed to providing a smooth and reliable payment and delivery experience across Cameroon.

1. Payment Methods

We accept the following payment options:

  • Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money)
  • Cash on delivery (available in selected locations)
  • Bank transfer (on request)

All payments must be confirmed before processing and delivery of orders.

2. Delivery Areas

We deliver across major cities in Cameroon including Douala, Yaoundé, Bamenda, and surrounding areas. Delivery to remote locations may take longer depending on accessibility.

3. Delivery Time
  • Major cities: 1 – 3 working days
  • Other regions: 2 – 5 working days

Delivery times may vary due to weather conditions, public holidays, or logistics delays.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery fees depend on your location and the size of your order. Final delivery cost will be communicated before dispatch.

5. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 24 hours after payment confirmation. You will be contacted via phone or WhatsApp for delivery arrangements.
