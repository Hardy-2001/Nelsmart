About Us

We model our renewable energy solutions towards ensuring longer energy conservation, green environment and cost effectiveness.

NELSMART is a leading renewable energy company in Cameroon. We specialize in solar and backup solutions for residential and commercial facilities.

Founded by a skilled team of engineers and energy experts, we aim to push the frontiers of clean and renewable energy. Our strength lies in our expertise, competence, and innovation.

We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient and affordable energy solutions across Cameroon and beyond.

Setting The Pace in an Evolving Industry

We design and supply solar panels, batteries, and hybrid inverter systems for off-grid, on-grid, and industrial applications.

Our solutions empower customers to deploy reliable energy systems tailored to their needs.

Our Vision ▼
To be Cameroon best-in-class renewable energy company, providing exceptional value through safe, reliable and innovative service.
Our Mission ▼
To deliver efficient, innovative and affordable solutions for eliminating energy poverty in Cameroon.
Our Core Value ▼
Professionalism, Excellence, Innovation, Integrity, Accountability, Teamwork and Safety.

Knowledge Sharing and Transfer

NELSMART technologies is committed to building a reputable renewable industry in Cameroon, by training aspiring and existing engineers or technicians.
