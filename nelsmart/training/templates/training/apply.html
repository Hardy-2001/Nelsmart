About the Academy

Nelsmart Solar Academy is the training arm of Nelsmart Technologies, one of the fast-growing renewable energy companies in Cameroon.

In partnership with leading global solar technology providers, we deliver intensive theoretical, practical, and real-life installation training in solar energy systems.

Our trainings are conducted in a conducive learning environment with experienced professionals and hands-on practical sessions to ensure real understanding.

Sessions are highly interactive, allowing trainees to ask questions, practice, and build confidence in real-world applications.

At the end of the training, participants receive certification and may be considered for partnership opportunities as certified Nelsmart installers.
